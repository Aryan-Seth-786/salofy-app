Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

In short: We do not sell or share your personal data with other companies for their marketing. We use your information to run the app and, when helpful, to show you more relevant salons, services, and results.

This Privacy Policy explains how GLAZZ (“GLAZZ”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the GLAZZ mobile app and related services (the “Service”), including our website at glazz.in.

1. Who we are

GLAZZ is a salon discovery and booking app. You can browse salons and services, book appointments, and manage your bookings and profile.

Contact for privacy questions: privacy@glazz.in

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Account details — such as your phone number, name, and profile preferences (for example city or gender preference if you choose to set them).
  • Booking activity — salons, services or packages you view or book, appointment times, and related status (confirmed, cancelled, rescheduled).
  • Location-related information — such as a city you select, or approximate location if you allow location access, so we can show nearby salons and deals.
  • Device and usage information — basic technical data needed to run and improve the app (for example app version, device type, and crash or error logs if enabled).
  • Communications — messages you send us through help or support.

We only ask for what we need to provide the Service. Some features (like nearby results) work better if you share location; you can still use the app by choosing a city manually where that option is available.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and secure your account (including phone OTP sign-in).
  • Show salons, services, deals, and search results — including more relevant results based on things like your city, preferences, past bookings, or favourites.
  • Process and manage bookings, reminders, and related notifications you allow.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Respond to support requests and protect against fraud or abuse.
  • Meet legal obligations when required.

4. We do not share your data for others’ marketing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal data with third parties for their advertising or marketing.

Your booking details may be visible to the salon you book with, so they can fulfil your appointment. That is part of providing the Service, not selling your data.

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of GLAZZ, our users, or the public.

5. Keeping your information safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data in transit and at rest (including HTTPS for our services). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above — for example while your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for bookings history, support, fraud prevention, or legal requirements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it where practical.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law (including India’s digital personal data rules where they apply), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

  • You can update profile details in the app where that option is available.
  • You can turn off non-essential notifications in device or in-app settings.
  • You can request account or data deletion by contacting privacy@glazz.in (and through in-app deletion when that feature is available).

We may need to verify your request and retain limited information when the law requires it (for example completed booking records).

8. Children

GLAZZ is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children for the purpose of offering the Service to them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy, except where applicable law requires additional consent.
